Questions

What happens to my images?

Your upload is held in server memory only long enough to produce the cutout, then dropped. Nothing is written to disk, and no copy is kept after the response is sent.

Which file types work?

PNG, JPEG, WebP and HEIC, up to 12 MB. The result is always a transparent PNG.

Will the download be watermarked?

No. When the site runs against a live API key the cutout is clean and full-resolution. A sandbox key — used for testing — returns a watermarked preview instead.

Can I put my subject on a new background?

Yes. Pick a colour or gradient, or upload your own image; compositing happens right in your browser, so switching backdrops is instant and costs nothing extra.
